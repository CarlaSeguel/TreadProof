## 6 Deliverable 1 Browser Signing and Application Integration

**Fixed deliverable allocation: US$1,400.**

We will implement the browser integration for the existing `create_batch`, `confirm_pickup`, `start_transit`, `confirm_reception` and `confirm_valorization` methods. Company signs creation; the assigned carrier signs pickup/transit; the assigned plant signs reception/valorization.

The implementation will:

1. Connect Freighter on desktop Chrome and require Stellar Testnet, the expected account, profile/organization/address matching and the batch-specific role before requesting a signature.
2. Fund fresh demonstration accounts through Friendbot. Each acting Testnet account pays its own transaction fees; Friendbot availability is a demo dependency. No production funding or custodial signing is included.
3. Integrate existing Supabase authentication and the API through the hosted application's same-origin route, replacing the current blanket Origin rejection with an explicit trusted-origin configuration.
4. Complete evidence upload before plant attestation; recover and verify the actual bytes/hash before passing that digest to the contract. An incomplete upload must remain incomplete and block that attestation request.
5. Simulate and assemble the transaction, request Freighter approval, submit the signed transaction to Stellar RPC, and poll for a confirmed result. Signing and successful submission are not final confirmation.
6. Persist the transaction hash/reference, network, contract ID, batch ID, action and confirmation state. Reconcile submitted/persisted references with chain state after interruption; a local record alone does not prove success.
7. Handle wrong account, wrong network, cancelled signature, timeout, retry and incomplete upload without false confirmed status or duplicate finalization.

Read-only presentation of existing ImpactRegistry credit status is included where shown. New reward-credit controls or badge issuance UI are not required.

**Planned Evidence of Completion:** source diff against the baseline, integration test results, role/action matrix, recorded successful signatures and confirmed transaction references, plus negative-case results for each listed error. Each artifact will identify the final release.

## 12 Differentiation and Boundaries

TreadProof is intentionally narrow: it focuses on NFU mass reconciliation across generating company, carrier and valorization-facility roles, rather than generic waste traceability. TrustedPlastic and TRAK are related-work references; no unsupported feature gaps or exclusive novelty are claimed.

Out of scope: new contracts or contract redesign; Mainnet; payments, USDC or escrow; IoT, scales, GPS or cameras; oracles; new badge levels or reward issuance UI; commercial tokens; carbon credits; legal/environmental certification; RETC/SINADER; physical treatment verification; industrial pilot; letters of interest; marketing/ad acquisition; external audit; broad onboarding; multiwallet/mobile/multisig/contract-account support; key recovery; batch splitting/merging; finalized-record correction; global physical deduplication; and production scaling.

Minimal new challenge/review/access records required for D3 are included, but do not change the existing contract architecture. A future pilot may test the operating procedure with real participants after the sprint; participation is not guaranteed.
